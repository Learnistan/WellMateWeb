If you need help now

Whatever brought you here, you do not have to hold it alone, and you do not have to explain it well. Start with the first thing on this page.

Right now, before anything else

These need no phone, no signal and no money.

  1. Put distance between you and anything that could harm you.If there is something within reach you could use to hurt yourself, move to another room, or ask someone to hold it for you.
  2. Slow your breathing out.Breathe in while you count to four. Breathe out while you count to six. Do it eight times. A longer breath out is what settles the body.
  3. Tell one person.It does not have to be the right person, or a professional. "I am not okay tonight" is enough of a sentence.
  4. Decide only about the next hour.You do not have to decide anything about your life tonight. Get to morning. That is the whole task.

People you can reach

We only list services we can verify. Where we cannot verify something, we say so rather than print a number that might not answer.

In Afghanistan

Free telephone counselling, in Dari and Pashto

Action Against Hunger has run a free national telephone counselling service since 2022, staffed by Afghan psychologists. Calls are free and confidential, and many come from provinces where there is no clinic nearby.

To be connected, ask at any health facility, clinic or community health worker for the Action Against Hunger psychosocial helpline. If you are already speaking to a health worker, they can call on your behalf.

For Afghans living in the United States

Afghan Behavioral Health Support

800‑615‑6514

Open 24 hours, every day. Counsellors speak Dari, Pashto and English. Free. Funded by the US Office of Refugee Resettlement. This number only works from inside the United States.

If someone's life is in danger

Call your local emergency number

If you or someone near you has been seriously hurt, or is about to be, contact local emergency services or get to the nearest hospital. That comes before anything on this page.

What we do not know. There is no single national crisis line in Afghanistan that we can point you to with confidence, and we will not invent one. A number that does not answer, at the moment you need it, is worse than no number at all. If you know of a service that should be listed here, please tell us.

If you cannot reach anyone yet

This takes about two minutes and works anywhere. Name things out loud, or in your head.

  • 5things you can see around you
  • 4things you can touch, and touch them
  • 3sounds you can hear
  • 2things you can smell
  • 1slow breath, longer out than in
